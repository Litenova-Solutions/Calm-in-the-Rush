# AI policy

AI assistance is allowed for code, tests, documentation, and design drafts.

Pull requests must disclose material AI assistance. Contributors remain responsible for correctness,
security, licensing, and test evidence. Do not submit secrets, private user information, or
confidential source material to AI services.

AI-generated media cannot enter the repository without provenance and license evidence. AI output
cannot approve or merge its own pull request. Fabricated citations, test results, and source
provenance are prohibited. AI-assisted contributions receive the same review as human-written work.
